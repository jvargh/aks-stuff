## Interpretation

- DNS-level evidence establishes resolver behavior only. `IMP-07`, `IMP-08`, `IMP-09`, `IMP-13`, and `IMP-14` remain proposed and blocked; they are not application-impact evidence.
- A 2-second client budget failed in this run. Even where another run succeeds at that boundary, it is not a safe margin.
- Forced TCP exercised adaptive dial behavior rather than the fixed UDP read-timeout path.
- Recovery wall time includes `kubectl exec` overhead and must not be treated as a resolver recovery SLA.
- The bounded load case demonstrates correct answers at the tested scale; it does not establish production capacity, tail latency, or workload SLO compliance.

## Cleanup proof

No fault NetworkPolicy or disposable resource remains because the entire `coredns-failover-validation-imp` namespace was deleted. The retained base lab and managed CoreDNS were healthy after deletion. No `kube-system` object was changed.
